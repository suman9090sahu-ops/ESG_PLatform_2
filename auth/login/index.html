Account Registration & Access Policy
Enterprise Role-Based Access Governance

The MEIL ESG Control Tower is an enterprise regulatory system. Self-registration is restricted by organization policy to ensure audit integrity and SEBI BRSR compliance.

Site Managers & Operators Account creation is provisioned by your Project Manager or Subsidiary Admin.
ESG Managers & Division Heads Credentials are issued directly through Corporate Sustainability Governance.
Assurance Officers & Auditors Independent audit access is authorized by the ESG Governance Committee.
To request platform credentials, email the Corporate ESG Office: sustainability@meilgroup.com
